Moderation // Enforcement

The Rules

Play hard, play fair, and do not make moderation necessary.

VIOLATION // CHEATING
Cheaters Get Hardware Banned

The client build is heavily hardened and the server simulates the authoritative game state at 128 Hz. Cheating is treated as a ban-level offense.

  • No Aimbots, Wallhacks, or ESPs.
  • No exploiting bugs for competitive advantage in ranked.
  • Confirmed cheating can lead to permanent HWID bans. Appeals are handled at discretion.
VIOLATION // TOXICITY
Don't Be A Jerk

Trash talk is part of the FPS DNA, we get it. But there is a line between competitive banter and being a miserable person.

  • No slurs, hate speech, or targeted harassment.
  • No doxxing or real-world threats.
  • No intentional team-killing or griefing your own squad.
  • If you cross the line, enjoy your silence (chat ban) or a vacation (temp ban).
SYSTEM // REPORTING
Reporting

If someone is breaking the rules, use the in-game report flow first. If you also have video evidence, send it directly to the developer through Discord.
