Privacy Policy

A plain-English summary of the data used to run the website, forum, and game services.

SECTION // COLLECTION
TL;DR We collect the information needed to run accounts, operate community features, store gameplay stats, and protect the service. We do not sell personal data.

1. What We Collect

When you play Block Shooter or use this site, we collect:

  • Account Info: Your Steam ID, username, email address, and forum profile details when you create or use an account.
  • Gameplay and Community Data: Match statistics, leaderboard data, forum posts, reports, and other content you choose to submit.
  • Technical and Security Data: IP address, device or session identifiers, and service logs used to keep the platform stable and investigate abuse.
SECTION // USAGE
TL;DR We use your information to run the service, support the community, and protect the platform from abuse.

2. How We Use It

We use this data to provide the services you are asking for. Specifically:

  • To authenticate accounts and let you sign in through supported providers such as Steam.
  • To run website, forum, matchmaking, and leaderboard features.
  • To investigate cheating, harassment, fraud, bugs, and other security or moderation issues.
SECTION // STORAGE
TL;DR We keep data for as long as it is needed to operate the service, enforce rules, and meet legal or security obligations.

3. Storage & Deletion

Account data, gameplay records, forum content, and moderation logs are retained for as long as they are needed to operate the service. Some security and enforcement records may be kept longer when necessary to prevent repeat abuse or comply with legal obligations. If you want to request account or forum-data deletion, please contact the developer through the project's official support channels.
